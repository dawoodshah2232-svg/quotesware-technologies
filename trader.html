Market & charting

The chart is the product face. It has to be trustworthy first.

QuotesWare is prioritizing feed continuity and candle integrity before decorative chart features. Every symbol should explicitly expose feed state and session state rather than silently drawing misleading data.

  • Bid/ask Market Watch with spread, daily high/low and quote age
  • M1 through monthly timeframes with stable candle aggregation
  • Candlestick, OHLC bar and line modes
  • Indicators, drawing objects and crosshair data window
  • SL/TP and trade levels visible directly on chart
