FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things every client asks before the first call.

How do we start a project together?

Send a few lines about what you're building through the form below. We'll come back with questions, then a clear proposal — scope, timeline and cost, in plain language.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, and we'll never guess before understanding it. As a rule of thumb: marketing sites and landing pages take weeks, custom platforms and apps take months. You get a real timeline in the proposal, and weekly builds you can click along the way.

Who owns the code and the product?

You do — completely. Everything we build is documented and handed over in full. Your product never depends on us to keep running.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. We'll help you cut scope to the smallest thing worth launching, ship it fast, and iterate from real user feedback instead of assumptions.

What happens after launch?

We monitor, fix and refine. Most clients stay with us on a maintenance rhythm — small improvements every month instead of one big rebuild every two years.

Which technologies do you build with?

We pick the stack per project from the tools we know deeply — React, Next.js, Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Flutter, Node.js and TypeScript. Boring, proven choices that your future team can hire for.
