  • Fast multi-timeframe charts with indicators and drawing tools

Developer platform

Integrate the brokerage, not just the terminal.

A modern platform needs explicit integration surfaces for CRM, portals, payments, analytics, liquidity and proprietary applications. QuotesWare is being structured around versioned APIs rather than hidden coupling.

  • REST and WebSocket API surface
  • Webhooks for account and trading events
  • FIX connectivity roadmap for institutional integrations
  • SDK and example-app documentation
  • Automation and AI-assisted operations roadmap
