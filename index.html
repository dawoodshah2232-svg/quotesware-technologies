FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things every brokerage asks before the first call.

Can we try the platform before committing?

Yes. Request a demo and we'll walk you through the Trader, Manager and Administrator in a guided session — on your use case, not a canned script.

Which platforms are supported?

Web terminal, Android app and Windows desktop today. iOS and macOS are on the roadmap.

Do you offer white-label?

Yes — currently in beta. Your app name, logo, colors, domain and module configuration, released through staged build channels on the QuotesWare core.

Where does market data come from?

The platform consumes real market-data feeds and always shows honest connection states — LIVE, DELAYED, STALE, CLOSED or OFFLINE. We never display simulated prices as live.

Is QuotesWare a broker?

No. QuotesWare Technologies provides trading software technology only. We don't hold client funds or provide investment services — licensing, liquidity and compliance remain the broker's responsibility.

What does implementation look like?

Discovery, branded build, integration, pilot, then launch. The deployment model is agreed per broker implementation.
