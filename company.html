Product principles

What QuotesWare should be known for.

Trust the data

Feed state, chart continuity and operational outcomes are explicit rather than cosmetically hidden.

Respect trader muscle memory

Professional traders should understand the product immediately, without forcing a legacy interface.

Give brokers real control

Trading conditions, permissions, brand and integrations belong in broker-facing products, not support tickets.

Integrate cleanly

External systems connect through contracts and adapters, not brittle assumptions inside the UI.

Label roadmap honestly

Preview, beta and future capabilities should never be marketed as already production-ready.

Design the whole ecosystem

Trader, Manager, Administrator and APIs must behave like parts of one product family.
